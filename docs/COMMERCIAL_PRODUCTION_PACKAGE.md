# Rapid Connect 58-second commercial production package

Status: production-ready brief. The in-app caption-first commercial remains the reviewable
fallback until final mastered media is approved.

## Deliverables

- `rapid-connect-58s-master-16x9.mp4` — 1920×1080, H.264, 25 fps, stereo.
- `rapid-connect-58s-social-9x16.mp4` — 1080×1920, safe text inside the central 80%.
- `rapid-connect-58s-square-1x1.mp4` — 1080×1080.
- `rapid-connect-58s-captions-en-au.srt` — burnt-in captions are also required.
- Clean music-and-effects mix and a separate voice-over stem.
- Thumbnail/end-card stills for outreach previews.

No real customer name, phone number, address, provider credential or production dashboard data may
appear in footage.

The read target is 122–132 words per minute, warm Australian delivery, practical rather than
futuristic. “AI” must sound like the letters A-I.

## Audio

- Voice-over stays intelligible on a phone speaker and peaks below −3 dBFS.
- Music is confident and modern without alarms, sirens or novelty “robot” sounds.
- Ring, message and confirmation effects support the story but never cover speech.
- Final integrated loudness target: approximately −14 LUFS for web delivery.

## Captions and accessibility

- Every spoken word is captioned.
- No caption line exceeds 42 characters where practical; use no more than two lines.
- Captions sit inside platform-safe areas and maintain WCAG AA contrast.
- Every claim needed to understand the offer is visible without audio.
- Do not rely on red/green alone to communicate missed/answered status.

## Claim and price controls

- Use “helps stop losing good jobs”, not an absolute earnings or job-volume guarantee.
- Show ongoing platform and usage pricing before the final signup action.
- The setup-fee waiver must be presented as a limited founding-plumber promotion.
- Do not imply that AI handles emergencies, diagnoses plumbing faults or replaces emergency
  services.
- Final end card must include: “Usage charges apply from activation. Normal platform billing
  starts after three monthly billing periods. Switching off does not cancel billing.”

## Review gate

The master is accepted only when:

1. All three aspect ratios show the same approved claims and prices.
2. Missed-Call Recovery and AI Receptionist are visually distinct, including whether the original
   call is missed or answered.
3. The lead shown after each interaction matches the customer inputs.
4. Captions, voice-over and on-screen actions remain synchronized after export.
5. The landing page records start, 25%, 50%, 75%, completion and close events.
6. Playback exits to the original split experience and the signup action works.
7. Mobile playback, reduced motion, keyboard controls and silent playback are usable.
